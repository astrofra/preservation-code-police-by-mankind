Code Police / Mankind — browser preservation

Upload ALL contents of this ZIP, preserving case and subdirectories, to any
static HTTP(S) host. Open index.html through HTTP(S), not file://. Subdirectories
and paths with spaces/Unicode are supported. No Java, CDN, PHP, remote media,
SharedArrayBuffer, COOP or COEP configuration is needed.

Serve .wasm as application/wasm and .js as text/javascript. The launch page is
UTF-8. Keep original/MKD_CodePOlice.txt (ISO-8859-1 bytes) unchanged.
For local preview after extraction: python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open http://localhost:8000/ .

Choose the artwork size, optional original cinema surround, fullscreen and sound,
then click Start. Return to the landing page to change resolution or sound and
restart. The cinema toggle is also available during playback. Escape exits
browser fullscreen first; Escape in the page or Accueil returns to the menu.
The original close image also returns to the menu. Hidden tabs pause the demo.

The artwork is always calculated at 520x300 and enlarged with nearest neighbour.
Windowed sizes fit within the requested bounds and available browser space.
Fullscreen maximizes the chosen presentation (whole cinema if enabled, otherwise
the image), centered on black, without changing monitor resolution.
